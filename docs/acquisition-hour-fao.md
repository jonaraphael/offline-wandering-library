## Checks already completed

Every new original starts with the PDF signature; the recorded exact byte count and SHA-256 were computed from its full body. `pdfinfo`, complete `pdftotext -layout` extraction, and all-page low-resolution Poppler rasterization exited successfully for all 2,364 pages. Thirty-six selected pages were visually inspected, including rights notices, feeding/storage tables, crop-water calculations, tool illustrations, silo plans and postharvest diagrams. This is file integrity and sampled visual QA, not a page-by-page editorial or veterinary/agronomic review.

Nonfatal source warnings were retained as evidence: greenhouse and handtools have JPEG2000 codestream warnings, and silos has font-weight warnings. Their sampled illustrations/tables render correctly. Some runs also reported sandbox Fontconfig cache paths unwritable. The dairy volume is the publisher’s legacy web-print PDF: a few figures cross PDF page boundaries, with continuation preserved. No PDFs were modified or repackaged.

Regional and historical scope matters: greenhouse guidance is for Mediterranean climates; the field-school manual is for coastal/delta Myanmar; the rabbit manual is the 1997 revision; dairy Volume 1 is the 1993 work in the 2008 electronic edition, not the entire six-volume series. The UC Davis original contains a December 2016 disclaimer that some practices may not meet current food-safety/regulatory standards. That notice and its edition date are preserved in the record and original.

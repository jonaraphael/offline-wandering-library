# Practical resource selections — 28 September 2026

Six complete publisher PDFs were selected for **full-1tb**, adding **76,927,328
bytes**. The smaller presets retain their prior selections. Each new asset has
a reusable YAML pseudoindex in `catalog/navigation/assignments/` with topic
routes, ordinary-language aliases and a review basis. Files without verified
downloads remain outside an assembled drive's search and atlas.

| Selected resource | Brief evaluation | Limits retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Building Soils for Better Crops, 4th edition](https://www.sare.org/resources/building-soils-for-better-crops/) | Adds soil structure, organic matter, compaction and fertility depth beyond the compost guide. | US examples; local soil and climate interpretation still needed. |
| [Crop Rotation on Organic Farms](https://www.sare.org/resources/crop-rotation-on-organic-farms/) | Practical field-planning worksheets and crop-sequence examples. | Strongest regional fit is northeastern US/eastern Canada; older economic examples. |

| [Manage Weeds on Your Farm](https://www.sare.org/resources/manage-weeds-on-your-farm/) | Illustrated identification and ecological/mechanical management of agricultural weeds. | US arable-crop focus; not a lawn, forest or permanent-pasture handbook. |
| [Managing Cover Crops Profitably, 3rd edition](https://www.sare.org/resources/managing-cover-crops-profitably-3rd-edition/) | Species comparisons and management tables complement the broader soils text. | Dated prices and input references; adapt species and timing locally. |
| [Trail Construction and Maintenance Notebook](https://www.fs.usda.gov/t-d/pubs/htmlpubs/htm07232806/index.htm) | Illustrated paths, drainage and field-maintenance techniques extend the hand-tool shelf. | 2007 field guide; not a road or structural-bridge standard. |

Python downloaded unchanged files, computed exact SHA-256 values, opened their
PDF structures and prepared bounded samples. Editorial review used publisher
descriptions/contents, the first six physical pages, one midpoint and the final
page; covers and midpoint pages were also inspected visually. This is a brief
selection review, not an exhaustive content or page-by-page certification. No
full-text index or guessed page links were created.

SARE offers free digital downloads, but this pass did not establish blanket
redistribution rights. Its five assets retain `redistributable: false` and all
publication notices. The Forest Service publication retains its government-work
and credited-material notice. Downloaded bodies and sampled images stay in the
ignored `.owl/practical-discovery-20260928/` directory, outside Git.

Three other leads remain unselected: FEMA P-232 exceeded the initial 40 MB
transfer bound; the earlier Wood Handbook attempt was rejected as encrypted;
the revised 2026 Organic Seed Alliance page did not offer a finished PDF link
in this check. The older seed PDF was not relabelled as the current revision.

Chapter 1: A reproducible library

A manifest lists approved source files and their checksums. A builder copies those files onto a drive and creates navigation pages. Verification compares each file's current checksum with its recorded checksum.

Review exercise

Why does saving a checksum separately help? If both the stored document and its adjacent checksum are replaced, comparing only those two cannot reveal the change.

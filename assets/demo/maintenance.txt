# OWL demonstration: library maintenance

This is a software test fixture, not a repair manual.

## Integrity

Verify the SHA-256 checksums after a copy. Store a second verified SSD separately.
Disconnect the drive safely after writing and keep its connector protected.

## Electrical and mechanical reference shelf

The real catalog includes electrical circuit textbooks and a mechanical
maintenance handbook. This fixture only exercises Markdown text extraction.

OWL contributors dedicate this original fixture to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

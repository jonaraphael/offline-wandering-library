How it works

  1. Plug in a drive.

    Choose a USB drive or SD card in the library builder.

  2. Press Sync.

    OWL selects files that fit, downloads them and verifies what it saves.

  3. Take it offline.

    Open START_HERE from the drive to browse your library.

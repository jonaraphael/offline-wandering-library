    Coverage and search help

    Search matches catalog titles, topics, aliases, descriptions, and approved chapter locations. It does not read document body text or recognize words in images. Try a topic or open a relevant book to search inside it.

    Exact titles and aliases appear first, followed by results matching all query words and then partial matches. Use up to 32 different words. Nothing is uploaded; all data and scripts stay on this drive.

    See discovery coverage and the inventory. Archive results identify their source and internal location; open them with a compatible reader. Static indexes remain available if your viewer blocks scripts.

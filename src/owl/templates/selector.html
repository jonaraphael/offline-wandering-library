A whole library on one drive.

Plug in a USB drive or SSD. This page measures it, picks the most useful books, guides and archives that fit, and builds the library straight onto the drive. After that it works anywhere, with no internet.

Any drive from 16 GB to 1 TB. Nothing already on it is erased.

Which drive should I use?

Any USB flash drive or SSD from 16 GB to 1 TB. Bigger drives hold more: Wikipedia with pictures starts fitting at 256 GB. Format the drive as exFAT so Macs, Windows PCs, Android phones and iPhones can all read it. Use Chrome, Edge or Arc on a computer for this page.

Plan a library for your drive.

Pick a drive size, adjust what goes on it, then copy one command for the desktop builder. This page works offline and downloads nothing.

  1. Choose your drive

    The page measures the free space, so the library is sized to fit.

  2. Review your library

    Survival essentials come first. The rest of the space goes to repair guides, school courses, maps, Wikipedia and books. Change anything you like.

  3. Press Build

    Every file downloads straight onto the drive and is checked. Search and a start page are added last.

The finished drive opens on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad.

How sizes are estimated

Known uncompressed sizes take precedence. Otherwise, rough assumptions are 3× stored size for ZIMs and 2× for ZIPs and reader packages. Actual ratios vary with the mix of text and media; these are planning estimates, not measured sizes. PDFs, HTML and other ordinary files use their original file size, including extracted PDFs. This does not decode images or PDF internals, or change what the builder stores. Reader and retained source packages are counted separately; metadata and free-space reserves are excluded.

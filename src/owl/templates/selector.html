    How it works

    Offline Wandering Library puts books, manuals and courses on a drive so you can read them without internet.

    Sync downloads and checks the files for your library, then builds a start page and search for the drive.

    Use a computer with an internet connection. Chrome, Edge and Arc can sync straight to a drive. In Firefox and other supported desktop browsers, build locally and extract the download onto your drive.

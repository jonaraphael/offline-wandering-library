How to open a book

Click or tap a category, then a title. If nothing opens, this is only a preview: open START_HERE.html in a web browser instead.

For a searchable catalog, close this page and open START_HERE_FOR_IPHONES.pdf on the drive in Preview. Tap the magnifying glass and enter a title or filename.

Or tap a category below. Touch and hold the filename and choose Copy. Close this page, paste the name into Files search and select your drive below the search field. If there’s no match, follow the folders shown with the name.
